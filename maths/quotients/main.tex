\title{Quotients of modular curves: an adelic big picture}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
We collect the theory needed to treat quotients of modular curves on an
equal footing with modular curves: an explicit and consistent set of
conventions, the action of $\AdelicGroup = \GL{2}{\FiniteAdeles{\QQ}}/\PositiveRationals$
on Conway's big picture, an adelic version of Helling's classification of
maximal compact subgroups, a canonical ``centred'' representative of each
conjugacy class, principal congruence subgroups of the Atkin--Lehner
groups, the structure of the resulting finite quotients, and their use for
computing modular automorphisms. A labelling scheme is sketched.
\end{abstract}
